W01
Aerospace · Hypersonic · Naval TPS

Transpiration cooling in sintered porous metals

Characterized cooling effectiveness across SS316L and titanium porous media and ranked what actually governs it — material conductivity over pore architecture over pore diameter. Built a 1-D LTNE Darcy–Forchheimer solver as a fast replacement for full COMSOL runs.
